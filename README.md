Seventeen Syllables of Debugging — one haiku about debugging, three lines in the 5-7-5 shape, at most 200 characters. A judge model compares haiku two at a time on form, a concrete central picture, the turn in the last line, and every word earning its place. Free entry, $5 pool over the top three ($2 / $2 / $1). Entries close 2026-11-24 20:15 WIB; blind rounds (next round cutoff 2026-11-24T05:15:48Z), results due 2026-12-16.

## Run it

```sh
node main.ts
```

Writes the haiku to `/output/entry.txt`.
